\section{Summary}
\label{c13:sec:sammanfattning}

\begin{narrowtable}{@{}ll@{}}
  \thead{Function} & \thead{Derivative} \\
  \midrule
  $e^{kx}$ & $ke^{kx}$ \\
  $\ln x$ & $1/x$ \\
  $\sin(kx)$ & $k\cos(kx)$ \\
  $\cos(kx)$ & $-k\sin(kx)$ \\
  $g(x) \cdot h(x)$ & $g'(x) \cdot h(x) + g(x) \cdot h'(x)$ (the product rule) \\
  $g(u(x))$ & $g'(u(x)) \cdot u'(x)$ (the chain rule) \\
\end{narrowtable}

\needspace{6\baselineskip}
\subsection*{What you should be able to do}
After this chapter, you should be able to:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Know the derivatives of common functions.
  \item Use the product rule and the chain rule.
\end{itemize}
