  \midrule
  Root, power & $\sqrt{x}$, $x^y$ & \code{sqrt(x)}, \code{pow(x, y)} \\
  Logarithms & $\ln x$, $\lg x$ & \code{log(x)}, \code{log10(x)} \\

\subsection*{What you should be able to do}
After this chapter, you should be able to:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Understand the difference between integers and floating-point numbers in C, and choose the
    right type and format specifier.
  \item Use \file{math.h} to calculate powers, roots, logarithms and decibels.
  \item Calculate with angles in radians and convert to and from degrees.
  \item Calculate a derivative numerically and compare it with the analytical derivative.
  \item Calculate a definite integral numerically with the trapezoidal rule.
  \item Represent complex numbers and phasors with \file{complex.h}, and calculate magnitude and
    argument.
  \item Calculate sample values of a sinusoidal signal and print them for further analysis.
\end{itemize}
