\chapter{Number systems and basic arithmetic}
\label{c1:ch}

{\large\itshape The numbers we calculate with, the order we calculate in, and the first
circuit.\par}

\begin{chapterbox}{In this chapter}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Number sets: natural numbers, integers, rational numbers and real numbers.
  \item The number line and absolute value.
  \item Basic arithmetic and the order of operations.
  \item Fractions, decimals and percentages.
  \item Resistors in series and in parallel, and Ohm's law.
\end{itemize}
\end{chapterbox}

This chapter lays the foundation for the rest of the book. It goes through the number sets that
mathematics builds on, how numbers are placed on the number line and what the absolute value of a
number is, the order in which operations are carried out, and how to calculate with fractions,
decimals and percentages. Here you already meet the first application in electrical engineering:
resistors in series and in parallel, and Ohm's law, where fractions and the order of operations are
used to calculate resistance, current and voltage in a simple circuit.

\Secref{c1:sec:talmangder} to \secref{c1:sec:procent} are the chapter's theory, with examples.
\Secref{c1:sec:sammanfattning} summarises the formulas and what you should be able to do after the
chapter, and \secref{c1:sec:uppgifter} holds the exercises. Most of them can be done in your head
or with pencil and paper.
