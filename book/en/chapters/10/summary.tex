\section{Summary}
\label{c10:sec:sammanfattning}

\begin{booktable}{@{}p{14em}L@{}}
  \thead{Concept} & \thead{Formula} \\
  \midrule
  Exponential equation & $a^x = b \;\Rightarrow\; x = \frac{\log b}{\log a}$ \\
  $\log(a^n)$ & $n \cdot \log a$ \\
  Voltage gain (dB) &
    $G_{\text{dB}} = 20\log\left(\frac{U_{\text{out}}}{U_{\text{in}}}\right)$ \\
  dB to linear gain & $G_{\text{lin}} = 10^{G_{\text{dB}}/20}$ \\
\end{booktable}

\needspace{6\baselineskip}
\subsection*{What you should be able to do}
After this chapter, you should be able to:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Calculate with, and solve equations with, logarithms and exponential functions.
  \item Understand what decibels are.
\end{itemize}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Solve $4^x = 64$.
  \item An amplifier has $G_{\text{dB}} = 40\,\text{dB}$. Calculate the linear voltage gain
    $G_{\text{lin}}$.
\end{enumerate}
