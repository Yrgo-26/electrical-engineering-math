\chapter{Review: vectors, functions, trigonometry and logarithms}
\label{c11:ch}

{\large\itshape Four chapters in one sweep, and a test to measure yourself against.\par}

\begin{chapterbox}{In this chapter}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Review of Chapters~\ref{c7:ch}--\ref{c10:ch}: vectors, functions, trigonometry,
    logarithms and decibels.
  \item Review exercises, one part for each chapter.
  \item Practice test~2, with the answers in the answer key.
\end{itemize}
\end{chapterbox}

This chapter has no new theory. Instead, it gathers up what was covered from \kapref{7} to
\kapref{10}: vectors and phasors, functions and their domains and ranges, sinusoidal AC voltages,
and logarithms and decibels. All of this is used in the chapters that follow, when the derivative
is introduced, so it pays to sort out now whatever still feels uncertain. Revise the theory in
Chapters~\ref{c7:ch}--\ref{c10:ch} before you start, and preferably do the review exercises on
your own.

\Secref{c11:sec:sammanfattning} states what you should be able to do after the chapter.
\Secref{c11:sec:uppgifter} holds the review exercises, arranged in one part for each chapter, and
\secref{c11:sec:ovningsdugga} practice test~2, which is set out like a real test. Take it on your
own, with pencil, paper and a calculator, and then compare with the answer key.
