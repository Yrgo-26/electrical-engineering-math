\section{Summary}
\label{c5:sec:sammanfattning}

\begin{booktable}{@{}p{12.5em}L@{}}
  \thead{Concept} & \thead{Key formula} \\
  \midrule
  Product rule & $a^m \cdot a^n = a^{m+n}$ \\
  Quotient rule & $a^m / a^n = a^{m-n}$ \\
  Power of a power & $(a^m)^n = a^{mn}$ \\
  Negative exponent & $a^{-n} = 1/a^n$ \\
  Square root & $\sqrt{a} = a^{1/2}$ \\
  Fractional exponent & $a^{m/n} = \sqrt[n]{a^m}$ \\
  Scientific notation & $a \times 10^n$, $1 \leq a < 10$ \\
  Electrical power & $P = U^2/R = RI^2$ \\
\end{booktable}

\needspace{6\baselineskip}
\subsection*{What you should be able to do}
After this chapter, you should be able to:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Know and apply the laws of exponents.
  \item Calculate and simplify expressions with roots.
  \item Write numbers in scientific notation and handle significant figures.
\end{itemize}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Simplify $\dfrac{(3x^2)^3}{9x^4}$ with the laws of exponents.
  \item A capacitance is $C = 220\,\text{nF}$. Give the value in farads in scientific notation.
\end{enumerate}
