\chapter{Trigonometric functions}
\label{c9:ch}

{\large\itshape Angles in degrees and radians, the sine function and the equation of an AC
voltage.\par}

\begin{chapterbox}{In this chapter}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Angle measure: degrees and radians.
  \item The sine function and its properties.
  \item The AC voltage equation: amplitude, frequency, period and phase.
  \item Reading the properties of an AC voltage from a graph.
\end{itemize}
\end{chapterbox}

This chapter is about the trigonometric functions, and above all the sine function, which
describes the AC voltage of the mains and the signals in almost all electronics. The chapter
begins with the angle measures degrees and radians and how to convert between them, then goes
through the properties of the sine function and shows how a sinusoidal AC voltage is described by
its amplitude, angular frequency and phase angle. Finally, you read the amplitude, period,
frequency and phase of an AC voltage from a graph and write its equation.

\Secref{c9:sec:vinkelmatt} to \secref{c9:sec:typexempel} are the chapter's theory, with examples.
\Secref{c9:sec:sammanfattning} summarises the formulas and what you should be able to do after the
chapter, and \secref{c9:sec:uppgifter} holds the exercises. Part~1 of them reviews earlier
chapters. Several of them need a calculator that can work in radians.
