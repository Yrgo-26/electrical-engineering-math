\section{Summary}
\label{c16:sec:sammanfattning}

\begin{booktable}{@{}p{12.5em}L@{}}
  \thead{Concept} & \thead{Formula} \\
  \midrule
  Euler's formula & $e^{j\delta} = \cos\delta + j\sin\delta$ \\
  Euler form & $z = \abs{z}e^{j\delta}$ \\
  Multiplication & $z_1z_2 = \abs{z_1}\abs{z_2}\,\angle\,(\delta_1 + \delta_2)$ \\
  Division & $z_1/z_2 = (\abs{z_1}/\abs{z_2})\,\angle\,(\delta_1 - \delta_2)$ \\
  From phasor to time domain & $u(t) = \abs{U}\sin(\omega t + \delta)$ \\
\end{booktable}

\needspace{6\baselineskip}
\subsection*{What you should be able to do}
After this chapter, you should be able to:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Understand Euler's formula.
  \item Know the difference between a complex quantity and a phasor.
  \item Understand how complex numbers can be used in vector calculations.
\end{itemize}
