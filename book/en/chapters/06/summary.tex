\subsection*{What you should be able to do}
After this chapter, you should be able to:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Understand when an equation is of the second degree.
  \item Solve quadratic equations with the PQ-formula.
  \item Solve quadratic equations by completing the square.
\end{itemize}
