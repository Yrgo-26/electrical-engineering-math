\section{Practice test 1}
\label{c6:sec:ovningsdugga}
The practice test covers Chapters 1--6 and prepares you for test~1, which is set out the same way
and has the same rules. The answers are in the answer key, and full worked solutions, in Swedish,
in \file{lectures/L06/appendix/practice_test/solutions.md}.

\paragraph{Permitted aids} Writing materials and any calculator, and the formula sheet used in the
course. Relevant formulas are written on the board.

\paragraph{Other information} The test does not affect the grade or the examination in the
course, but it can give 0.5~bonus points on the regular exam. The bonus requires at least 3.5 of
the maximum 5.0~points. Every question requires a solution with a stated answer, including the
unit where needed. Good luck!

\begin{aside}
\note[Note] Mobile phones may not be used while the test is in progress and must be left in the
place indicated.
\end{aside}

\prov{od1}

\provuppgift{1}{1.0}
The resistance of two resistors $R_1$ and $R_2$ in parallel can be calculated with the formula
\[
  \frac{1}{R} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2}.
\]
Calculate the resistance $R_2$ if $R_1 = 10\,\text{k}\Omega$ and the parallel resistance is
$R = 4.5\,\text{k}\Omega$. Give the answer in k$\Omega$ to one significant figure.

\begin{aside}
\note[Note] $\Omega$ is the unit of resistance and is read ‘ohm’.
\end{aside}

\provuppgift{2}{1.0}
Simplify the following expression as far as possible:
\[
  \frac{\dfrac{c^8 - c^4}{c^2}}{c(c^2 - 1)}
\]

\provuppgift{3}{1.0}
Solve the system of equations below. Give the answers exactly, as fractions.
\[
  \begin{cases}
    4y - 2x + 6 = 0 \\
    3y = 5x + 4
  \end{cases}
\]

\provuppgift{4}{0.5}
The equations in \hyperref[od1:u:3]{question~3} are examples of the equation of a straight line.
Give the slope $k$ and the $y$-intercept $m$ of the first equation.

\provuppgift{5}{0.5}
A resistor with the resistance $R = 4.7\,\text{k}\Omega$ carries the current
$I = 2.5\,\text{mA}$. Calculate the power $P$ using powers of ten:
\[
  P = RI^2
\]
Give the answer in watts in scientific notation, to two significant figures.

\provuppgift{6}{1.0}
Solve the following equation:
\[
  8(x + 2)^2 - 5(x - 3)^2 = 4 - 20x
\]
Give the answer to two decimal places.
