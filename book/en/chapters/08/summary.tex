\section{Summary}
\label{c8:sec:sammanfattning}

\begin{booktable}{@{}p{12.5em}L@{}}
  \thead{Concept} & \thead{Explanation} \\
  \midrule
  Function & Every $x$ gives exactly one $f(x)$ \\
  Domain & Permitted $x$-values \\
  Range & Possible $f(x)$-values \\
  Linear function & $f(x) = kx + m$ \\
  Exponential function & $f(x) = C \cdot a^x$ \\
\end{booktable}

\subsection*{What you should be able to do}
After this chapter, you should be able to:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Recall the key concepts of functions from compulsory school and Mathematics~1.
  \item Understand the concept of a function.
  \item Recognise the common types of function.
  \item Use functions and graphs to solve problems.
\end{itemize}
