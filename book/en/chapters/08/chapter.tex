\chapter{Functions}
\label{c8:ch}

{\large\itshape One value in and exactly one value out: the function, its graph and the most common
types.\par}

\begin{chapterbox}{In this chapter}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The concept of a function: a rule that gives exactly one value for each permitted $x$.
  \item Domain and range.
  \item Common types of function: linear, power, exponential and polynomial functions.
  \item Reading a graph, and solving problems with functions and graphs.
\end{itemize}
\end{chapterbox}

This chapter is about functions, one of the most important tools in mathematics. A function is a
rule that gives exactly one function value for every permitted value of $x$, and it can be
described by a formula, a table or a graph. The chapter reviews the concepts from compulsory school
and upper-secondary Mathematics~1: domain and range, the most common types of function and what can
be read from a graph. In electrical engineering, functions describe how one quantity depends on
another, for example how the resistance of a resistor depends on the temperature or how the power
in a resistor depends on the current.

\Secref{c8:sec:funktion} to \secref{c8:sec:typexempel} are the chapter's theory, with examples.
\Secref{c8:sec:sammanfattning} summarises the concepts and what you should be able to do after the

and paper, but a few need a calculator.
