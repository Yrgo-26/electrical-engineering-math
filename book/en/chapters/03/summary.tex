\section{Summary}
\label{c3:sec:sammanfattning}

\begin{booktable}{@{}p{10em}L@{}}
  \thead{Concept} & \thead{Key rule} \\
  \midrule
  Linear equation & Isolate the unknown with the addition and multiplication principles \\
  Proportion & Cross-multiplication: $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d} \Rightarrow ad = bc$ \\
  Inequality & The same rules as for an equation, but the sign reverses when you multiply or
    divide by a negative number \\
  Absolute value & $\abs{ax + b} = c \Rightarrow$ two cases: $ax + b = c$ or $ax + b = -c$ \\
\end{booktable}

\needspace{6\baselineskip}
\subsection*{What you should be able to do}
After this chapter, you should be able to:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Solve linear equations algebraically.
  \item Understand and solve inequalities.
  \item Understand and calculate the absolute value of a number or an expression.
  \item Set up and solve equations and inequalities from electrical relationships.
\end{itemize}

\testadigsjalv
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Solve the equation $4(x - 2) = 2x + 8$ and check the answer.
  \item The voltage is $U = 3.6\,\text{V}$ and the resistance $R = 120\,\Omega$. Calculate the
    current $I$ with Ohm's law.
  \item The terminal voltage of a loaded battery is $U = 9 - 0.5I$. Find the currents that
    satisfy $U \geq 6\,\text{V}$.
\end{enumerate}
