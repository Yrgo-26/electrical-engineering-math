\chapter{Review and exam preparation}
\label{c19:ch}

{\large\itshape The whole course once more, and an exam to practise on.\par}

\begin{chapterbox}{In this chapter}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Review of the course content before the exam.
  \item How the exam is organised: format, permitted aids, points and grade thresholds.
  \item A practice exam covering the whole course, with answers in the answer key.
\end{itemize}
\end{chapterbox}

The course ends with a written exam, and this chapter prepares you for it. There is no new
material here. Instead, the chapter describes how the exam works, and then comes a practice exam
that tests the central parts of the course: arithmetic, algebra, equations, trigonometry,
logarithms, derivatives, integrals and complex numbers, that is, more or less everything you have
calculated with in Chapters~\ref{c1:ch} to \ref{c17:ch}.

\Secref{c19:sec:infor-tentamen} goes through the exam's format, the permitted aids, the points
and the grade thresholds, and how to prepare. \Secref{c19:sec:ovningstentamen} holds the practice
exam. Sit it on your own first, under the same conditions as the exam: with only the permitted
aids and without looking at the answer key. Then mark it against the answer key at the back of
the book, and after that go through the worked solutions, in Swedish, published in the course
repository, for the questions where you went wrong or got stuck. \Secref{c19:sec:sammanfattning}
closes the chapter, and the course, with a few questions to reflect on.
