\section{Summary}
\label{c19:sec:sammanfattning}

\subsection*{What you should be able to do}
After this chapter, you should:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Be prepared for the exam.
  \item Have an overall understanding of the central parts of the course.
\end{itemize}

\needspace{6\baselineskip}
\subsection*{Reflect}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Which area of the course do you feel least sure of? Write down a specific question about it
    and find out the answer before the exam.
  \item Solve a question from the practice exam without aids, and then compare with the worked
    solution.
  \item Go through the question you found hardest in the practice exam, and explain the method of
    solution to a fellow student.
\end{enumerate}

\needspace{6\baselineskip}
\subsection*{End of the course}
The course ends with the written exam described in \secref{c19:sec:infor-tentamen}. Thank you for
following the course, and good luck!
