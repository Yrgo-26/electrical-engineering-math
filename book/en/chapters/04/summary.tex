\section{Summary}
\label{c4:sec:sammanfattning}

\begin{booktable}{@{}p{12.5em}L@{}}
  \thead{Method} & \thead{Best when} \\
  \midrule
  Substitution method & One unknown is easy to express in terms of the other \\
  Elimination method & The coefficients are integers and the elimination is straightforward \\
\end{booktable}

\noindent The steps are the same in both methods:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Set the system up clearly.
  \item Solve for one unknown.
  \item Calculate the other unknown.
  \item Check in \emph{both} equations.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{What you should be able to do}
After this chapter, you should be able to:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Understand what a system of equations is and when it is used.
  \item Solve linear systems of equations by the substitution method.
  \item Solve linear systems of equations by the elimination method.
\end{itemize}
