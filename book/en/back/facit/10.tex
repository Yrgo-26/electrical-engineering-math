\svar{1.3} $u(t) = 4\sin\left(50\pi t - \frac{\pi}{4}\right)\,\text{V}$

\svar{1.4} $u(t) = 4\sin\left(100\pi t - \frac{\pi}{6}\right)\,\text{V}$. The curve has the period
$T = 20\,\text{ms}$ and starts at $u(0) = -2\,\text{V}$. It passes upwards through zero at
$t \approx 1.67\,\text{ms}$ (a twelfth of a period late), reaches the peak $4\,\text{V}$ at
$6.67\,\text{ms}$ and the trough $-4\,\text{V}$ at $16.67\,\text{ms}$.

\svar{1.5} $\delta \approx -3.25\,\text{rad}$ or $\delta \approx -1.15\,\text{rad}$,
exactly $-\frac{31\pi}{30}$ and $-\frac{11\pi}{30}$.

\svar{2.1} \sv{a} $3$ \sv{b} $10$ \sv{c} $\approx 3.79$

\svar{2.2} $t \approx 132.88\,\text{h}$, about $132$~hours and $53$~minutes.

\svar{2.3} $G_{\text{lin}} \approx 50.12$, that is, about $50$.

\svar{2.4} $\abs{U} \approx 10.0\,\text{V}$ ($U_{\text{RMS}} \approx 7.08\,\text{V}$).

\svar{3.1} \sv{a} $2$ \sv{b} $3$ \sv{c} $0$ \sv{d} $-2$ \sv{e} $1$ \sv{f} $0$

\svar{3.2} \sv{a} $\log 12$ \sv{b} $\log 5$ \sv{c} $\log 25$ \sv{d} $1$ \sv{e} $\log 2$

\svar{3.3} \sv{a} $5$ \sv{b} $-3$ \sv{c} $\approx 2.86$ \sv{d} $\approx 3.00$ \sv{e} $2$

\svar{3.4} \sv{a} $100$ \sv{b} $1$ \sv{c} $5$ \sv{d} $100$
\sv{e} $\frac{e}{2} \approx 1.36$

\svar{3.5} \sv{a} $40\,\text{dB}$ \sv{b} $0\,\text{dB}$ \sv{c} $-20\,\text{dB}$
\sv{d} Attenuation: the output signal is smaller than the input signal, so the ratio is less than
$1$ and its logarithm negative.

\svar{3.6} \sv{a} $10$ \sv{b} $1000$ \sv{c} $\approx 2.00$ \sv{d} $\approx 0.708$
\sv{e} $\approx 1.0\,\text{V}$

\svar{3.7} \sv{a} $20\,\text{dB}$ \sv{b} $\approx -3.01\,\text{dB}$
\sv{c} The power is proportional to the square of the voltage, $P = U^2/R$, and the exponent
$2$ becomes a factor in front of the logarithm: $10 \cdot 2 = 20$.
\sv{d} The power: $\approx 3\,\text{dB}$ ($3.01$). The voltage: $\approx 6\,\text{dB}$ ($6.02$).

\svar{3.8} \sv{a} $26\,\text{dB}$ \sv{b} $\approx 20.0$
\sv{c} $3.98 \times 7.94 \times 0.631 \approx 20.0$
\sv{d} Linear gains are multiplied but dB values are added, since
$\log(ab) = \log a + \log b$.

\svar{3.9} \sv{a} $0\,\text{dBV}$ \sv{b} $20\,\text{dBV}$ \sv{c} $-20\,\text{dBV}$
\sv{d} $\approx 3.98\,\text{V}$ \sv{e} $\approx 5.63\,\text{V}$

\svar{3.10} \sv{a} $a = 0.98$ \sv{b} $\approx 34.3\,\text{h}$ \sv{c} $\approx 7.3$ days
\sv{d} $C \cdot a^t = 2C$ gives $a^t = 2$, and taking logarithms gives $t\log a = \log 2$.

\svar{3.11} \sv{a} It is $\log(ab)$ that equals $\log a + \log b$; $\log(a + b)$ cannot be
simplified.
\sv{b} The quotient is a change of base: $\frac{\log 8}{\log 2} = \log_2 8 = 3$.
\sv{c} The exponent becomes a factor: $\log(a^n) = n\log a$.
\sv{d} $0\,\text{dB}$ gives $G_{\text{lin}} = 1$: the signal passes unchanged.
\sv{e} A power of ten and the base-10 logarithm cancel each other: $10^{\log x} = x$.
\sv{f} $\ln 0$ is undefined; it is $\ln 1$ that is $0$.
