\chapter{Answers}
\label{facit:ch}

This appendix holds the answers to all the exercises in the book, to the questions under
\emph{Test yourself} in each chapter, and to the practice tests and the practice exam. An
exercise's number is a link back to the exercise, and every exercise gives the page its answer is
on.

The answer key holds only the answers. An answer shows whether you got it right, but not how to
get there; the full worked solutions, in Swedish, are published in the course repository after
each lesson. Where an exercise asks you to show, justify or explain something, the answer key
gives the conclusion and the decisive reason, not the whole argument.

The answers are rounded as in the worked solutions. An answer that differs in the last decimal
place is most often because you rounded earlier in the calculation; calculate with unrounded
values and round only in the answer.
